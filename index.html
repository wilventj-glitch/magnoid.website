About Us

Magnoid is a student organization dedicated to developing leadership, teamwork, creativity, and service. We provide opportunities for students to grow through events, projects, and community engagement.

Vision

To inspire students to become confident leaders who create positive change in their school and community.

Mission

  • Develop leadership skills.
  • Encourage teamwork and responsibility.
  • Organize meaningful activities and events.
  • Promote service and community involvement.
