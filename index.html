How to Use MAGNOBOT

Step 1: Place MAGNOBOT on a flat surface.

Step 2: Turn on the power switch.

Step 3: Position MAGNOBOT in the area that needs cleaning.

Step 4: Allow MAGNOBOT to collect dust and debris.

Step 5: Empty the collection compartment after use.

Step 6: Recharge when the battery is low.
