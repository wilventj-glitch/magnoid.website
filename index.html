Our Products

MAGNOBOT is an innovative cleaning robot designed to make household cleaning easier, faster, and more efficient. Using smart movement and magnetic technology, MAGNOBOT helps collect dust and debris while reducing the effort needed to maintain a clean home.

How to Use MAGNOBOT

Step 1: Place MAGNOBOT on a flat surface.

Step 2: Turn on the power switch.

Step 3: Position MAGNOBOT in the area that needs cleaning.

Step 4: Allow MAGNOBOT to move and collect dust and debris.

Step 5: Empty the collection compartment after use.

Step 6: Recharge the device when the battery is low.
